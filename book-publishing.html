Common Challenges in Book Publishing

Publishing a book involves much more than writing it. Many authors face technical, creative, and marketing challenges that can delay or limit their success. Here are the key obstacles that make the book publishing process difficult for most authors.

Formatting & Technical Issues

Incorrect layout, font usage, or print setup can cause file rejections or poor-quality book interiors on publishing platforms.

Unprofessional Cover Design

Many authors struggle to create a visually appealing and platform-compliant cover that attracts readers and meets print specifications.

Lack of Editing & Proofreading

Skipping professional editing can lead to grammatical errors, weak structure, and readability issues that harm your credibility as an author.

Poor Marketing & Visibility

Without proper keywords, descriptions, and promotions, even great books can go unnoticed in today’s competitive marketplace.

Pricing & Distribution Confusion

Authors often struggle to set the right price, understand royalties, and choose the best distribution channels for global reach.

Time-Consuming Process

Coordinating editing, design, formatting, and publishing steps can take weeks — especially without professional guidance and project management.

Yes. You retain 100% ownership and rights to your work. Our team simply helps you publish it professionally and efficiently.
