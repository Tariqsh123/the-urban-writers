Submission Guide

Follow these quick and easy steps to send your book sample to The Urban Writers for a free trial edit. Our editorial team will carefully review your submission and get back to you within 48 hours.

01

Fill Out the Form

Enter your full name, email address, and phone number so we can reach you with updates and editor feedback.

02

Provide Book Information

Add your book title, choose the correct genre, and describe your project — including tone, target audience, or any special requirements.

03

Upload Your Sample

Attach a short sample of your manuscript. This helps our editors assess your writing style and assign the best match for your book.

04

Submit for Review

Once submitted, our editors will review your sample within 48 hours and send back a professionally edited excerpt — completely free.

05

Review & Decide

Take your time to review the free sample edit. You’ll only proceed and pay once you’re completely satisfied with our work.
