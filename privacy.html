Privacy Policy

Welcome to The Urban Writers. Your privacy is very important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit or use our website and services. It works alongside our Terms and Conditions.

In this Privacy Policy, the terms "we," "us," "our," and "service" refer to The Urban Writers, located at www.theurbanwriters.com. By using our website, you agree to the terms outlined here regarding data collection, sharing, and storage.

The information we collect helps us improve our services, provide a better user experience, and communicate relevant updates. By using our website, you consent to the collection and use of data as described in this policy.

Information Collection and Use

The Urban Writers collects certain information to improve our services. This may include:

Personal Data

We may collect personal information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Cookies and usage data

We collect this data through forms, newsletters, service interactions, or promotional materials. You may choose not to provide some information, but it could limit certain services.

Usage Data

When you visit The Urban Writers, we may automatically gather usage data such as:

  • IP address
  • Date and time of visit
  • Pages visited and duration
  • Other diagnostic or analytics data

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to understand user interactions and enhance our website. You can disable cookies via browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

  • Session Cookies
  • Preference Cookies
  • Security Cookies
  • Advertising Cookies

Other Information

We may also collect data necessary to provide our services, including:

  • Demographic details (age, gender, etc.)
  • Educational or professional background
  • Employment agreements or relevant legal documents
  • Account or subscription details

Purpose of Data Collection

  • Improve service quality and user experience
  • Send updates about services or changes
  • Provide customer support
  • Analyse website usage and trends
  • Address technical or service issues
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Send newsletters, promotions, or offers (with consent)

Data Storage

We retain personal information only as long as needed to provide services or comply with legal obligations. All data is securely stored and handled responsibly.

Data Transfer

Data may be shared with trusted partners under strict security protocols. In cases of mergers or business transactions, your data may be transferred under the same privacy protections.

Disclosure of Data

Other Situations

  • With your consent when necessary

Data Security

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Third-Party Services
