Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about publishing with The Urban Writers.

What services do you offer? ▼
We provide full-service solutions including writing, editing, cover design, formatting, publishing, and marketing to help your book reach its full potential.
How long does the publishing process take? ▼
The timeline varies based on the services you select and your responsiveness, usually ranging from 4–12 weeks for most projects.
Do I retain the rights to my book? ▼
Absolutely! You keep 100% ownership. We provide guidance and professional services, but your intellectual property always remains yours.
Can I choose my own cover design? ▼
Yes! Our design team works closely with you to craft a cover that perfectly represents your story and appeals to your readers.
Do you help with marketing my book? ▼
Yes, we offer marketing strategies including social media promotion, book listings, and SEO optimization to help your book reach more readers.
Can I publish both digital and print versions? ▼
Definitely! We support eBooks, paperbacks, and hardcover formats, making your book available in both digital and physical forms.
Is there a satisfaction guarantee? ▼
Yes. You only pay when you’re fully satisfied with our editing, design, and publishing services. We strive for 100% client satisfaction.
